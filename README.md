## About

I'm **Baccar Elourajini**, a web developer, programmer, entrepreneur, and lifelong learner.

I enjoy transforming ideas into practical digital products and exploring the space where **technology, education, and entrepreneurship** meet.

My work is guided by a simple cycle:

**Learn → Build → Improve → Share → Teach**

I believe technology becomes more valuable when it solves real problems and creates opportunities for people to learn, work, and grow.

# My Approach

I don't believe good development starts with code.

It starts with understanding the problem.

```text
       UNDERSTAND
            ↓
          EXPLORE
            ↓
          DESIGN
            ↓
           BUILD
            ↓
           TEST
            ↓
         IMPROVE
            ↓
          SHARE
            ↓
           LEARN
            ↺
```

Every project is both a product and a learning experience.

# Technology × Education

Education is one of the areas where I believe technology can make a meaningful difference.

Through **CFK School**, I explore how digital tools can support:

```text
Teachers
    ↓
Better preparation
Better resources
Better communication
        ↓
Students
    ↓
Better learning
More practice
More engagement
        ↓
Parents
    ↓
Better visibility
Better communication
Better support
```

The objective is not to replace education with technology.

It is to use technology to **strengthen the learning ecosystem**.
